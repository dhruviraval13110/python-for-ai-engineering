## 2. The mental model

Think of **Accessibility Design and Misleading Charts** as a system with inputs, transformations, assumptions, outputs, and failure modes. Before touching an API, write down those five things. This habit transfers directly to data analysis, machine learning, and production engineering.

A strong engineer asks:

- What problem am I solving?
