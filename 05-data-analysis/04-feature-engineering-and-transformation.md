## 1. Learning outcome

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to explain **Feature Engineering and Transformation** without relying on memorized definitions, implement a small version from scratch, identify common failure modes, and decide when the technique is appropriate in a real system.

## 2. The mental model

Think of **Feature Engineering and Transformation** as a system with inputs, transformations, assumptions, outputs, and failure modes. Before touching an API, write down those five things. This habit transfers directly to data analysis, machine learning, and production engineering.

A strong engineer asks:

- What problem am I solving?
- What is the unit or shape of the data?
- What assumptions am I making?
- What can go wrong?
- How will I measure correctness?
- How will I reproduce the result?
- How will another engineer understand this later?
